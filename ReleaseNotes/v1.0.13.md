# iBestSpeech 1.0.13

Fourteen commits since 1.0.12, and two of them are worth the update on their
own.

**The app now has an icon.** 1.0.12 shipped without one — the archive had no
`Assets.car` and `Info.plist` never declared `CFBundleIconName`, which is what
App Store Connect rejects with ITMS-90022. It installs with a proper icon now.

**The preview no longer stops dead.** The ellipsis fix already ran in the
VoiceOver extension, but the app's own preview handed the engine the text as
typed — so the same notification spoke correctly in VoiceOver and stopped in
the preview, which is the path you test with. The preview now runs the same
text layer.

**You can load a whole NVDA addon.** Instead of finding the right DLL inside
an addon by hand, pick the `.nvda-addon` itself and the voices come out of it.
An addon is an ordinary zip, and the archives disagree about where a DLL
lives — depth 1 to 6, some Windows-built with backslashes where the spec wants
slashes, one keeping its engine outside `synthDrivers/`, four of the twelve on
this machine containing no DLL at all, and the largest one belonging to a PDF
library. So nothing is decided by name, path or size: every candidate is handed
to the engine and kept only if it actually speaks. Verified against the real
archives — the BestSpeech addon yields all thirteen of its voices, and the four
without a Keynote Gold engine yield nothing, with no false positives.

**The About screen shows the real version and build**, and the all-caps section
headers are gone — the engine spells an all-caps run letter by letter, so
"Your voice files" was being read out as a string of capitals by the app's own
voices.

Also in this release: the signed App Store build and TestFlight upload path,
and the extension's version can no longer drift from the app's, which is an
upload failure waiting to happen.

The public build still carries no voice data. The tables belong to the people
who made them; you import your own Keynote Gold file, and the files you load
stay on your device.
