# iBestSpeech 1.0.11

One speech fix from listening, measured against the engine's own audio on
all three English builds.

## What changed

**Leading dotted names say "dot" instead of going silent.** A letter-dot
combination at the start of what is being read -- "claude.ai",
"9to5google.com", "google.com" -- produced zero audio on every build. The
text layer now says the word "dot" in exactly that case ("claude dot A I",
"9 to 5 google dot com"). Dots mid-sentence, in URLs, and in abbreviations
like "e.g." already spoke and are left untouched, as are digit dots in
decimals and version numbers. The exposed "ai" reads as "A I" via its own
new dictionary entry (alone it read as "aye").
