# iBestSpeech 1.0.9

Three dictionary fixes from listening, each measured against the engine's own
audio on all three English builds.

## What changed

**bottleneck reads as "bottle neck".** The joined word voices its double-t
and flattens its middle; splitting gives the clean standalone words.

**PizzaCat reads as "pizza cat".** The camel case read as one unknown word;
the ordinary word "pizza" (usual "ts") plus "cat" is what was asked for.

**Devin reads as "Dev in".** The joined word voices its "e" long ("Divin");
"Dev" is the short-e form, as in "developer" and "Kevin", with "in"
unstressed.
