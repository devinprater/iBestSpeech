# iBestSpeech 1.0.8

Two dictionary fixes from listening, both measured against the engine's own
audio on all three English builds.

## What changed

**Reddit reads as "red it".** The split "Red dit" gave the second syllable
the full vowel of "bit", with its own stress: "red DIT". "dit" rhymes with
"bit" in this engine, while "it" is the reduced function-word form -- so
"red it" stresses RED the way the word is stressed.

**The Safari entry is gone.** Its split "Sa fari" read as "essay Barry" on
2006ENG ("A-Fairy" on 1998ENG, "SA Perry" on 1995), while the engine reads
native "safari" correctly on all three builds. The entry was making it worse,
so it was deleted. The rest of the brand splits were re-audited at the same
time: TikTok stays ("Tik" and "Tok" are byte-identical to "tick" and "tock"),
Discord stays ("diss card" on 1995 is the closest available spelling), and
the remainder transcribe correctly.
