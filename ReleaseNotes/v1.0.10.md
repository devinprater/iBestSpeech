# iBestSpeech 1.0.10

One dictionary fix from listening, measured against the engine's own audio
on all three English builds.

## What changed

**Stange reads as "stanggy".** The joined word drops its "g" entirely
(transcribes as "Saint"). The single word "stanggy" keeps "stang" plus a
hard-g "ee" ending, in one token with one stress -- right for a surname.
